Ask back

Select it.
Ask it.

Stuck on a line in a book, a paper or a long thread? Select it, hold the smart read key and ask, out loud. Let go, and the answer is spoken, grounded in what you're reading.

  1. 1Select what you're reading

    A phrase, a paragraph or a whole page, in any app.

  2. 2Hold ⌥, and ask

    In your own words: “What does that mean?”

  3. 3Let go and listen

    A short answer in a few sentences. Ask again; it remembers the text.
