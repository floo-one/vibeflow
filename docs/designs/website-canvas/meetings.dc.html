Only real meetings.

A calendar is full of things that aren't meetings. VibeFlow skips them, judged by your own email, even on a colleague's calendar you watch. A meeting on two of your calendars shows once.

  • Focus time
  • Out of office
  • Working location
  • All-day events and birthdays
  • Declined or cancelled
  • Marked as free
  • Just you, nobody else

Set up in Settings › Meetings

  1. Sign in with Google. Read-only.
  2. Pick the calendars to watch.
  3. Choose the lead time: 5, 10 or 15 minutes.

It syncs every five minutes, after your Mac wakes and when the network comes back. A status line says whether you're signed in, offline, or blocked by your Workspace admin.

Your calendar stays yours

VibeFlow asks Google for read-only access and keeps events in memory on your Mac. Nothing goes to a VibeFlow server, because there isn't one. The sign-in lives in your Keychain.

Sign out in Settings › Meetings, or remove VibeFlow at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

My work account says it's blocked.

Some Google Workspace admins only allow approved apps. Ask yours to allow VibeFlow, or use a personal account.

Can I turn it off?

Yes, with one switch in Settings › Meetings. Like every tool in VibeFlow, it is an on/off and nothing more.

Comes with a voice, too.

Meetings is one of VibeFlow's tools. The app's main job is voice: Option and three keys to read, explain and dictate. See how it works.
